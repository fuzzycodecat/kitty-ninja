# 🐱 Kitty Ninja vs Zombies! 🧟

A fun, action-packed browser-based game where you control a ninja kitty defending against waves of zombies!

## 🎮 Play Now

**[Click here to play the game!](https://fuzzycodecat.github.io/kitty-ninja/)** or open the `index.html` file in your browser.

## 🕹️ How to Play

### Controls
- **Mouse Movement** - Move your kitty ninja around the screen
- **Mouse Click** - Shoot your current weapon at zombies
- **SPACE Bar** - Switch between different weapons
- **P Key** - Pause/Resume the game

### Game Mechanics
1. **Defend Your Position** - Zombies will attack from all directions
2. **Switch Weapons** - Press SPACE to cycle through your arsenal
3. **Survive Waves** - Each wave gets progressively harder with more zombies
4. **Track Your Score** - Try to get the highest score possible!

## 🎯 Features

- **Multiple Weapons** - Switch between different attacks:
  - ⭐ **Star** - Classic projectile weapon
  - 🔥 **Fire** - Spread damage attack
  - ⚡ **Laser** - Powerful beam weapon
  - 💣 **Bomb** - Area of effect damage
  
- **Progressive Difficulty** - Waves get tougher as you survive longer
- **Real-time Combat** - Smooth, fast-paced action gameplay
- **Score Tracking** - Compete with yourself to beat your high score
- **Wave System** - Clear waves to unlock new challenges
- **Game Over Detection** - Know when you've been defeated by the horde

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- **Learn Your Weapons** - Each weapon has different properties; experiment to find your favorite
- **Positioning** - Use the mouse to stay away from zombie clusters
- **Weapon Switching** - Switch weapons strategically based on the zombie formation
- **Stay Mobile** - Continuous movement is key to survival
